## 0.6.0

- Calls like WhatsApp: `GraineCalls.instance` puts your agent in a native call — the customer calls it (`startCall(reason:, variables:)`, which returns the call's id, or null while another call is live), or your agent rings the customer through a push: CallKit and VoIP pushes on iOS (`enableVoIPPushes`), a ringing call notification and call screen on Android (`handlePush(message.data)` from your Firebase handlers, foreground and background). The call's audio runs natively, with the platform's echo canceller, and goes on with the screen off.
- `configure(GraineCallsOptions(...))` once at launch: `publishableKey`, `displayName`, `ringtoneSound`, `ringTimeout`, `appVersion`, `callScreen` (`sdk`, the default: the SDK's call screen; `app`: yours), `androidSmallIcon`, `iosIconTemplate`, `iosIncludesCallsInRecents`. The options are kept natively, so a push that wakes the app rings before any Dart runs.
- `attach()` hands the call what the app tells the agent through `GraineInApp` — `identify`, `setVariables`, `setScreen` and `registerAction` — kept in step, with the agent's actions answered by the app's own handlers.
- `outcomes` (answered, declined, missed, ended with local · remote · failed, failed with the reason) and `incomingCalls` streams, for your server; `GraineCalls.instance` is a `ValueListenable<GraineCallSnapshot>` (`status`, `callId`, `direction`, `reason`, `callerName`, `muted`, `speakerOn`, `agentSpeaking`, `caption`, `connectedAt`, `error`) for a call screen of your own, with `answer()`, `decline()`, `end()`, `setMuted()` and `setSpeaker()`.
- One call at a time: a native call ends the WebView engine's call, and `voice.start()` refuses during one.
- The package is now a Flutter plugin with Android and iOS code: adding it needs a new store build.
- **Android manifest:** the plugin adds what a call needs — `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE`, the call screen, its microphone service and receiver. On Android 14+, Google Play asks apps that declare `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT` or `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE` to explain them. An app that does not use calls removes those two permissions and `ai.graine.inapp.GraineCallService` with `tools:node="remove"` in its own manifest (snippet in the Flutter guide).

Guide: [In-app calling → Flutter](https://www.graine.ai/docs/in-app/calling#flutter).

## 0.5.0

- A connect that says where it is (contract/DESIGN.md, "Connecting"). The engine now reports a connect's stage with `graine:progress` — registering, dialling, retrying (the first attempt went unanswered; on mobile data, usually a connection the phone dropped while idle, being replaced), switching (moving to the other transport) or failed — and the voice bar says it: a retry or a switch is the loader's "reconnecting" moment, in the brand's own words from Appearance → Loading; a connect the customer has waited on for 4 s (their bar on screen while the page loads, or a call being placed) says "Still connecting…" — but only while the brand's connecting words are the default "Connecting…": a wording the brand chose, often not English, is never replaced. When nothing could carry the call the bar stops connecting and says "Couldn’t connect · tap to retry" (a curly apostrophe), and the microphone places a new call. A stage this SDK does not know is ignored.
- `GraineVoice.connectStage` (`ConnectStage.registering`, `dialling`, `retrying`, `switching`, or null outside a connect), `slowConnect` and `callFailed`, as the `GraineConnectProgress` interface beside `GraineCallState` — so a host's own call state keeps compiling, and a bar drawn from one that does not report progress draws exactly as before. `callFailed` clears on a new start, a connected call, and the last bar leaving the screen; the host hears `onCallState(false, false)` when a connect fails. `GraineTiming.slowConnectMs` (default 4000, `ConnectProgress.slowConnectMs`); `viewOpened()` / `viewClosed()` for a host drawing its own bar (the SDK's bar calls them itself) — an idle page loading behind no bar is nobody's slow connect. `voiceBarStatus` takes `callFailed`, `connectStage`, `slowConnect` and the brand's `connectingText`; `connectingLook` gives the connect's words alone, and `BarStatus.label` the words the loader says in place of the brand's ("Still connecting…" only).
- The brand's logo, whole, on its plate (contract/DESIGN.md, "The brand's logo"). Wherever the logo stands in for the launcher glyph — the voice bar's avatar, the chat header's mark, the launcher — it is fitted (`BoxFit.contain`) into the largest square its shape holds, centred and never cropped: 70% of the size for a circle, a squircle and a pill's height, 80% for a rounded square and a square, rounded down (26 in the 38-point avatar, 39 or 44 in the 56-point launcher, 21 or 24 in the 30-point mark). The avatar drew it with `cover` across the circle, which cut a wordmark down to its middle and a square logo's corners off. It sits on the plate the dashboard chose, `appearance.logoPlate`: `accent` — the shape's own fill, as before, and the default — or `white`: #FFFFFF with a `max(1, borderWidth)` outline in `border`, a pill's label then in #101318 (a neo-brutalist launcher keeps its `colors.text` outline; without a design, the white plate's hairline is #E5E7EB). A glyph, a letter and a host's own `avatarUrl` stay on the accent; a logo that cannot load leaves the letter, in ink readable on its plate.
- `NativeAppearance.logoPlate` (`LogoPlate.accent` or `LogoPlate.white`; exactly "white" is white, anything else the accent — the same as the React Native SDK), and `BrandLogo.box`, `BrandLogo.plateLook` and `LogoPlateLook` for hosts drawing their own.
- Tested against the six new design philosophies (Swiss Grid, Precision, Japandi Calm, Fintech Clear, Bauhaus, Gamified): every design draws the bar, the launcher, the transcript and a card from its own tokens, light and dark.

## 0.4.0

- Window chrome: the chat, caption and components open as desktop windows when the design says so — Desktop Buddy does. With the design's `chrome: "window"` (contract/DESIGN.md, "Window chrome"), a title bar runs across the top of each surface: solid `surfaceRaised` — even when the design is glass — with a `borderWidth` outline below it, under the surface's own top corners; 32 high on the chat window and components, 24 on the caption card. The traffic lights sit 12 in from the left (10 across, 8 on the caption, 6 apart, always #FF5F57, #FEBC2E and #28C840), and the title is centred on the whole bar on one line, in the platform's monospace at the caption size, `textMuted`, regular weight, lower case — clear of the lights. The chat window's title bar replaces its header: the heading, after the 6-point `success` dot while online and alone while offline, and the collapse chevron 12 in from the right end; no launcher mark. The caption card reads "captions". A component card's own title moves into its bar and is not drawn again in the body (its rows keep theirs; with no title, the lights stand alone), and its body is `surface`, so the `surfaceRaised` bar reads as a title bar — the white window under the grey bar (a host's own `GraineWidgetTheme.surface` still wins, for the body and the bar alike); the loader card and the answered line are unchanged. Every other design — and a server that does not send `chrome` — keeps the plain surfaces.
- `ThemeTokens.chrome` (`"none"` or `"window"`; absent or unknown is `"none"`) and `ThemeTokens.window`; `WindowChrome` and `ChromeSurface` carry the title bar's numbers for hosts drawing their own.

## 0.3.0

- The voice bar is the Garage's design, element by element (contract/DESIGN.md, "Voice bar"): a 60-point bar on the design's surface — translucent over a blur with glass — with its outline, `radius.xl` corners and the floating shadow, or the neo-brutalist solid offset, never blurred; the accent avatar with the launcher glyph (else the logo filling it, else the name's first letter); the name and the status line in the design's type; a four-bar meter beside "Listening" and "Speaking" that moves at the design's pace and stands still without motion or under reduce motion; the microphone in the accent (danger when muted, at 40% while it cannot act) that starts, mutes and retries; the end button that hangs up during a call and closes off one (`onClose`). The status words are the contract's, in its order and in sentence case — "Unavailable", "No connection", "Reconnecting…", "Needs microphone", "Couldn’t load · tap to retry", "Connecting…", "Muted", "Thinking…", "Speaking", "Weak connection", "Listening", "Tap to talk" — with the dashboard's loader and its words while the call connects, reconnects or the agent thinks. The caption card (with "YOU" over the customer's own lines) and the chat window rise into place at the design's pace.
- Icons in the design's style: the SDK's microphone, hang up, send, chat, call, chevron and close are painted from the dashboard's own path data, in the design's icon style (outline, sharp, filled, duotone) and stroke — no emoji anywhere on the bar.
- The design's typeface, loaded by the SDK: the faces a design draws with are fetched from `/api/embed/font` on the SDK's base URL, kept in the app's cache directory by URL (fetched once), registered with `FontLoader` and drawn as their own weight on the bar, the caption, the chat window, components, the launcher and the transcript. Until a face arrives — and if it never does — text is in the platform font at the design's weight. `GraineConfig.fonts` now lists families the app bundles itself, which win and are never fetched; `GraineInApp.fontFaces` names what has loaded.
- A glass surface paints its shadow only outside itself, as the dashboard's preview does, so a translucent bar never shows its own shadow through it.
- `GraineAgentBar`: `onClose`, `showCaptions`, and — for an agent offered as both a call and a chat — `chat` (a `GraineChatState` your app runs), `surface` and `onSurfaceChange`, which add the switch and the chat window (header, bubbles in the design's style, composer). `GraineVoice.phase` and `GraineVoice.error` (a misconfigured transport, said as "Unavailable"); `voiceBarStatus`, `callPhase`, `DesignFonts` and `SdkIcons` for hosts drawing their own.

## 0.2.0

- Draws the brand's design. The session now carries the design chosen in the dashboard, resolved into light and dark tokens; the bar, components and loaders draw from them (surfaces, outlines, corners, shadows or a solid offset, glass, type, motion, chips, buttons), picking light or dark as the brand fixed it or as the device is — and switching with the device. From a server without designs, everything looks exactly as before.
- `GraineLauncher`: a floating button in the design's shape and glyph. `GraineTranscriptView`: the conversation so far, in the design's bubbles.
- `GraineConfig.fonts`: the font families your app bundles. A design's font is used only when listed; otherwise the platform font.
- `GraineInApp.design`, `GraineInApp.tokens` and `tokensListenable`, for a host drawing its own UI.

## 0.1.1

- Back in the foreground, an idle engine is told to get ready again (`graine:warm`). The engine keeps its line ready so a tap costs only the call itself, but a phone drops that idle connection while the app is in the background. The SDK now tells the engine when the app returns — only when the page is ready, never during a call.
- Connecting is faster in the hosted engine itself — a warm tap connects in about 0.2 s — with no change to this package.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- `GraineEngine` hosts Graine's voice engine once, above the navigator, so a call outlives every route.
- Screen context (`setScreen`), prompt variables (`identify`, `setVariables`) and in-app actions (`registerAction`), masked on the device before anything leaves it.
- Components the agent draws — product lists in five looks, plan comparisons, choices, calendar slots — with the dashboard's loader while one is on its way.
- `GraineAgentBar`: draggable, keyboard-aware, and says what the network is doing ("weak connection", "no connection", "reconnecting…").
- Tested against the same contract as the React Native, Android and iOS SDKs.
